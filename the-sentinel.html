Built from 3oz vegetable-tanned chestnut, skived to half thickness at every fold so the wallet closes flat from the first day and keeps closing flat in ten years. Six card slots sit staggered for quick reach; the bill pocket runs the full width.

Nothing is lined. The inside of a hide is honest, and it burnishes to a soft, dark sheen where your thumb rests.

Made to order · vegetable-tanned where specified · repairs for the life of the piece
