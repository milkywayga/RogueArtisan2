Custom leatherwork, from first idea to finished piece.

Rogue Artisan works with customers in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland on custom leather goods and one-of-a-kind commissions. Shipping is available across Canada and the United States.

What can I commission?

Wallets, cardholders, small bags, travel accessories, desk pieces and other practical leather goods. If you have a different idea, describe it and we can discuss whether it is a good fit.

How does it work?

Start with an inquiry. We will discuss intended use, dimensions, leather, colour, construction and budget before confirming an estimate and whether the project can be scheduled.

Where do you ship?

Local inquiries are welcome from Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Finished commissions can be shipped within Canada and to the United States. Shipping to other countries can be arranged, at cost.

Each commission is confirmed individually. Materials, final pricing, timing and shipping arrangements are agreed before work begins.

Terms & Conditions

Every Rogue Artisan commission is individually designed and handcrafted. The following terms help establish clear expectations. By commissioning a piece or using this site, you're agreeing to the terms below.

1. Inquiries & Acceptance

Submitting a commission inquiry does not constitute a purchase, contract, or guarantee that a project will be accepted. Rogue Artisan reserves the right to decline projects based on design requirements, materials, scheduling, or other considerations.

A commission is considered accepted once the project details, pricing, estimated completion date, and deposit arrangements have been agreed upon. No deposit is taken until you're approved the final design and price. Once the deposit is paid and cutting starts, the refund policy applies (Section 6).

2. Pricing & Deposits

Pricing shown on this site (including those on the commission form) are estimates for standard options. Pricing for your commission determined by the design, materials, hardware, complexity, and time required to complete the piece. A quotation will be provided before a project is accepted and work begins.

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to reserve a place in the build queue and commence a commission. The remaining balance is due once the piece is completed and before it is shipped or handed over, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Any additional costs arising from requested changes or additions after approval will be discussed and agreed upon before proceeding.

3. Design Approval & Changes

Customers are responsible for reviewing and approving the agreed design details, including dimensions, layout, colour, leather selection, hardware, and other specifications.

Changes requested after approval may affect the final price and completion date. Once construction has begun, some changes may no longer be possible, these will be discussed with you.

Rogue Artisan will communicate any significant changes in scope, timeline, or cost before proceeding.

4. Materials & Handmade Variations

Leather is a natural material. Variations in grain, texture, colour, markings, and other characteristics are normal and contribute to the individuality of each piece.

As every item is made by hand, minor variations in stitching, dimensions, and finish may occur. Finished pieces may differ slightly from photographs, samples, or reference images.

These natural characteristics are not considered defects.

5. Production Times

Each commission is made individually and production times vary depending on the complexity of the project, material availability, existing workload, and life responsibilities outside of the workshop.

An estimated completion date will be communicated before work begins. While every effort will be made to meet that estimate, unforeseen circumstances may occasionally cause delays. Customers will be informed of significant changes.

6. Cancellations & Refunds

As commissions involve reserved production time and individually sourced materials, the 50% deposit is non-refundable. Finished custom commissions are not eligible for a refund or return once work has begun, except as described below.

If a customer wishes to cancel after work has begun, any remaining balance or refund will be assessed based on the work completed, materials purchased, and costs incurred. Any applicable consumer rights under law remain unaffected.

Defects and shipping damage. If your piece arrives with a manufacturing defect or genuine mistake on my part (stitching failure or hardware fault), contact me on Instagram or at RogueArtisan@outlook.com within 7 days of receiving it, with photos. We will assess the situation whether it can be repaired, replaced, or otherwise made right.

If Rogue Artisan is unable to complete an accepted commission, the customer will be contacted to discuss an appropriate resolution.

7. Shipping & Delivery

Local pickup or delivery arrangements may be discussed for customers in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Shipping is available within Canada and the United States. Shipping elsewhere can be arranged, at cost.

Shipping costs, applicable duties, taxes, and delivery arrangements will be communicated before dispatch. Customers are responsible for providing an accurate delivery address.

Once an order has been dispatched, delivery times and handling are subject to the shipping carrier. Any shipping issues should be reported promptly so they can be addressed.

8. Care & Use

Leather is a natural material that develops character through use and age. Regular care and appropriate storage will help preserve the condition of a finished piece.

Customers are responsible for using and maintaining their leather goods appropriately. Damage resulting from misuse, neglect, accidents, exposure to unsuitable conditions, or normal wear and tear is not considered a manufacturing defect.

Rogue Artisan may photograph finished commissions for portfolio and marketing use (site gallery, catalogue, instagram, etc) unless you request otherwise when placing your order.
