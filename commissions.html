Custom leatherwork · Vancouver, BC

Custom leatherwork, from first idea to finished piece.

Rogue Artisan works with customers in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland on custom leather goods and one-of-a-kind commissions. Shipping is available across Canada and the United States.

What can I commission?

Wallets, cardholders, bags, travel accessories, desk pieces and other practical leather goods. If you have a different idea, describe it and we can discuss whether it is a good fit.

How does it work?

Start with an enquiry. We will discuss intended use, dimensions, leather, colour, construction and budget before confirming an estimate and whether the project can be scheduled.

Where do you ship?

Local enquiries are welcome from Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Finished commissions can be shipped within Canada and to the United States.

Each commission is confirmed individually. Materials, final pricing, timing and shipping arrangements are agreed before work begins.

How it works

Start with the idea.

  • Send a description of what you're after.
  • We'll talk materials, size, details, and expectations.
  • I'll confirm whether it's a good fit and provide an estimate.
  • Once approved, your piece joins the build queue.
  • Made slowly: This studio operates as a dedicated craft bench, not an assembly line. Build times naturally fluctuate based on current project queues. Every client gets a clear target completion date before work begins but typical build times range from 4 to 6 weeks.
