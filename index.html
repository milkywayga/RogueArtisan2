Custom leatherwork · Vancouver & the Lower Mainland

Handmade leather goods, made with purpose.

Rogue Artisan creates custom leatherwork and handmade leather goods in Vancouver, British Columbia. From everyday wallets and cardholders to travel accessories, bags and one-of-a-kind commissions, each project is shaped around its intended use, materials and details.

Customers in Vancouver and across the Lower Mainland can enquire about a custom piece directly. Shipping is available throughout Canada and the United States.

Have an idea that is not represented in the catalogue? Get in touch about a custom leather commission and start a conversation about the design, leather, dimensions and finish.
